Interests

Reinforcement learning and decision-making; social learning, cultural evolution and cross-cultural cognition; human–AI interaction; machine psychology; AI safety and governance
Methods
Computational cognitive modelling (reinforcement learning, Bayesian inference, evidence-accumulation models); machine learning and NLP (text classification, clustering, sentence embeddings); quantitative analysis of large-scale and panel data, in Python and R

Personal and academic projects

  • 2026
    Do optimistic learners do better in social dilemmas?

    Extends Hoxha, Sperber and Palminteri (2025), which asks whether evolution favours positivity-biased learners, from bandit tasks to an iterated Prisoner's Dilemma with Q-learning agents.

    Code
  • 2025
    Narratives of distress

    Unsupervised NLP on around 800 expert-annotated posts from five mental health communities. Sentence-BERT clusters aligned with community boundaries and differed in emotional tone.

    Code
  • 2025
    Forecasting under structural breaks

    Regularised regression on 96 years of Yuan-dynasty inflation, where a structural break limited out-of-sample generalisation, and classifiers predicting Bank of England rate decisions.

    Code
  • 2024
    Housing price prediction

    An end-to-end pipeline on 179,000 Stockholm apartment sales, benchmarking linear, gradient-boosted and neural models. A tuned LightGBM reached 6.9% test MAPE.

    Code
